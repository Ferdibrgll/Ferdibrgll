## ⚡ About Me

I'm a **Cybersecurity Specialist** with hands-on experience in **network and security support**, **penetration testing** and **incident response**, backed by Cisco, Fortinet, HTB and APIsec certifications. I'm currently studying Computer Programming and hold a driver's license.

Beyond day-to-day work, I research **IoT & embedded device security**, **firmware analysis** and **wireless protocols** (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa), and I share what I learn through videos, articles and hands-on training.
